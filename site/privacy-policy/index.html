Privacy policy

Galen Marten Music respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how it’s protected.

Information we collect

When you contact us, sign up for emails, or place an order, we may collect:

  • Name, email address, and phone number
  • Venue details for booking inquiries
  • Payment details (processed securely through Stripe)
  • Information about your order when purchasing merch
  • Technical data like cookies and website analytics

How we use your information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to booking and contact requests
  • Send updates, newsletters, or event announcements (if you sign up)
  • Process and ship merch orders through Printful and Stripe
  • Improve the website experience and track performance with analytics

Third-party services

  • Printful handles merch printing and shipping.
  • Stripe processes payments securely — we never store your credit card information.
  • Netlify hosts this website and receives form submissions.
  • Spotify, YouTube, and Instagram may collect data through embedded players and links in line with their own privacy policies.

Your choices

  • You can unsubscribe from emails at any time.
  • You can disable cookies in your browser if you prefer not to be tracked.
  • To request that your data be deleted, please reach out through the contact page.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or how your information is handled, please contact us.
