About

Galen Marten

Galen Marten is a musician and songwriter who brings cinematic storytelling to life through music. As a one-man band, he weaves together guitar, harmonica, foot percussion, and soulful vocals to create a sound that’s as raw as it is authentic.

With decades of experience performing across the country — both solo and with full bands — Galen has developed a dynamic style that resonates with any audience. His setlists flow seamlessly between crowd-pleasing covers from every major genre and original songs rooted in Americana and folk rock. Known for his energy and connection with the crowd, Galen has a rare ability to turn any stage into an unforgettable experience.
