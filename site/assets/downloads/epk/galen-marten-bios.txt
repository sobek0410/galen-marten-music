FULL BIO
Galen Marten is an Ohio-based musician and songwriter with more than 30 years of experience and over 1,300 performances. His one-man-band show brings together guitar, harmonica, foot percussion, and vocals in a live set that moves between familiar favorites and original songs rooted in Americana and folk rock.

From patios and pubs along Lake Erie to festivals and private events, Galen builds his sets around the room and the audience. His repertoire spans classic rock, country, modern rock, and Americana, with a full song list available on his website. Solo, duo, and four-piece performance options are available by arrangement.

His recordings include Pictures on the Wall, The Blue EP, and the single Live Free, created for the documentary Am I Happy? His first live album, One Man Banned, is scheduled for November 28, 2026, with a release show at CODA in Tremont, Cleveland.
