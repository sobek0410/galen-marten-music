Am I Happy? is a feature documentary from Galen Marten and Freedomology Films that follows entrepreneur Greg Clement on a journey to uncover the true meaning of happiness. From conversations with neuroscientists and philosophers to candid moments with children and everyday people, the film explores timeless questions about purpose, fulfillment, and what it really takes to live a meaningful life. Scheduled for release this fall, Am I Happy? invites audiences to reflect on their own pursuit of joy.

The act

One man.
Every sound.

As a one-man band, Galen Marten performs guitar, harmonica, percussion with his feet, and vocals — a sound that’s both raw and authentic. Performing across Ohio, he draws on 30+ years of experience to cover everything from crowd favorites to deep cuts, all with a style that makes each song his own.
